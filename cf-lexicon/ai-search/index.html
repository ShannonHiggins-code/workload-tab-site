AI Search

⚙️ Product Spec

AI Search is WorkLoad Tab's universal search bar feature that toggles between Perplexity AI mode and Google Search mode directly from the tab bar — no extra tabs needed.

One search bar, two brains. Toggle the sparkle icon for AI answers or the magnifying glass for classic Google. It's like having a research assistant built into your new tab page.
