Bookmarks Popup

Impact: 70

⚙️ Product Spec

The Bookmarks Popup in WorkLoad Tab provides quick access to Chrome bookmarks directly from the new tab dashboard, allowing users to browse and open bookmarks without navigating to the bookmarks manager.

💬 User Voice

Your Chrome bookmarks, always one click away. No more digging through chrome://bookmarks or that tiny star menu. It's all right there on your dashboard.
