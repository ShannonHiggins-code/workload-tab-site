Open Tabs Panel

Impact: 75

⚙️ Product Spec

The Open Tabs Panel is a live panel on the far left of WorkLoad Tab showing all currently open Chrome tabs in real time. Tabs are dragged from this panel into Lanes to save them.

💬 User Voice

The tabs you actually have open, right there in front of you. Drag them into lanes before they multiply. It's tab taming made visual — see it, sort it, save it.
