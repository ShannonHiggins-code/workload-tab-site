Lane Launch

Impact: 82

⚙️ Product Spec

Lane Launch is the feature that opens all tabs saved in a Lane with one click using the rocket icon. Bulk tab restoration gets users back to their workflow state instantly.

💬 User Voice

The rocket button. One click and all your saved tabs blast open. Monday morning? Launch your work lane. Research session? Launch your reading lane. It's workspace teleportation.
