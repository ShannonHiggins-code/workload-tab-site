Lanes

Impact Score: 85

📋 Product Spec

Lanes are named containers within a workspace for grouping related browser tabs. Users drag open tabs from the Live Tabs panel into lanes to save them. Each lane can be named, reordered, color-coded, and launched in bulk.

💬 User Voice

Think of Lanes as filing cabinets for your browser tabs, except you actually use them. Group your research tabs, your shopping tabs, and your "I'll read this later" tabs that you'll never read.
